How to play GTA 5 on a Mac

Grand Theft Auto V has no Mac version, but its story mode has been played on Macs with Apple silicon, with the same Wine Portnaut uses, and Portnaut sets it up for free. GTA Online doesn't work: read what works first.

Story mode played on a Mac with Apple silicon (Highball's compatibility database) · Portnaut 0.12.0 · Updated 2 November 2026

What works, and what doesn't

  • Story mode works. The Steam copy of GTA V Legacy, the original DirectX 11 edition, has been played on Macs with Apple silicon on the DXMT renderer, as recorded in Highball's open compatibility database. Portnaut uses the same Wine. We haven't played it through Portnaut ourselves yet.
  • GTA Online doesn't work. Rockstar protects GTA Online with BattlEye, an anti-cheat that runs in the Windows kernel, and Wine can't run it, on a Mac or anywhere else. Story mode starts without it.
  • Install GTA V Legacy, not Enhanced. Steam lists the two editions separately. Legacy is the one played on a Mac.
  • Steam is the tested store. Copies from the Rockstar Games Launcher or the Epic Games Store haven't been tried yet.

What you need

  • A Mac or MacBook with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14.6 or later, and Rosetta.
  • GTA V on Steam, a Steam account and a Rockstar Games account.
  • Around 100 GB of free space for the game.

Play GTA 5 on your Mac, step by step

  1. Download Portnaut, open it, and choose Prepare Portnaut.
  2. Create a Steam bottle. Choose Bottles › New Bottle…, select Steam and keep easy mode on.
  3. Install Steam and sign in. Choose Install Steam, then Open Steam. The Steam guide has the details.
  4. Install Grand Theft Auto V Legacy from your Steam library. When the download finishes, the game appears in Portnaut's library by itself.
  5. Press Play in Portnaut. Easy mode switches the bottle to DXMT, the renderer GTA V was played on. Steam opens the Rockstar Games Launcher first: sign in with your Rockstar account, and it hands over to the game. The first start can take several minutes.

Portnaut sets two things up for you. GTA V Legacy gets the launch option -nobattleye when it joins your library, which starts story mode without BattlEye; you'll see it in the game's Settings…. And the Rockstar Games Launcher's sign-in window is set to draw, which on a Mac it otherwise doesn't.

Tips

  • Keep easy mode on, or set the bottle to DXMT yourself: the Rockstar Games Launcher doesn't start on D3DMetal.
  • Controllers work: GTA V sees them as Xbox controllers.
  • Saves: with easy mode, Portnaut backs up your saves before every session.
  • Slow? Lower the game's resolution or settings, or turn on MetalFX upscaling in the bottle's Graphics section. More performance tips.

Questions

Can you play GTA 5 on a Mac?

Yes, the story mode. GTA V has no Mac version, but the Steam copy of GTA V Legacy has been played on Macs with Apple silicon through Wine on the DXMT renderer, the setup Portnaut uses. You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

Does GTA Online work on a Mac?

No. GTA Online requires BattlEye, an anti-cheat that runs in the Windows kernel, and Wine can't run it. That's the same on a Mac, on Linux and on the Steam Deck. Only story mode works.

Which GTA 5 edition should I install?

GTA V Legacy, the original DirectX 11 edition. It's the one that's been played on a Mac. The Enhanced edition hasn't been tested yet.

Do I need Windows or Boot Camp?

No. Portnaut runs the game through Wine, which needs no copy of Windows. Boot Camp doesn't exist on Macs with Apple silicon.

Is it free?

Portnaut is free and open source. You need to own GTA V on Steam.
