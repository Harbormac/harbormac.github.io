Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon: M1 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • macOS 14.6 or later.
  • Rosetta, which Portnaut's Wine runs through. Portnaut tells you if it is missing (how to install it).
  • About 3 GB of free space to set up, plus room for your games, and an internet connection.

Install Portnaut

  1. Download Portnaut-{{version}}.zip from the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Open the zip and move Portnaut to your Applications folder.
  3. Open Portnaut. The first time, macOS stops it; see the next section.
  4. Choose Prepare Portnaut. Portnaut downloads Wine and the renderers once (a few hundred MB), checks each against its SHA-256 checksum, and sets them up in ~/Library/Application Support/Portnaut.
  5. Choose Bottles › New Bottle…, pick Steam, the EA app, Ubisoft Connect or an empty bottle, and follow the guide for your launcher.

“Portnaut” can't be opened: allowing it the first time

Portnaut is not notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app and doesn't open it. You allow it once:

  1. Try to open Portnaut, then close the message.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
  3. Next to the message about Portnaut, choose Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

From then on Portnaut opens like any other app.

Installing Rosetta

Rosetta lets Apple silicon Macs run apps made for Intel processors. The Windows compatibility layer Portnaut uses (Wine) runs through it, while Portnaut itself runs natively. If Rosetta is missing, Portnaut says so before setting up. To install it, open Terminal and run:

softwareupdate --install-rosetta

Accept Apple's licence when Terminal asks, then choose Prepare Portnaut again.

Check your download

D3DMetal is Apple's, from the Game Porting Toolkit, licensed for non-commercial use in developing, testing and evaluating games on Apple hardware. Portnaut is free and non-commercial and installs it unmodified.

Uninstall Portnaut

  1. Quit Portnaut and move it from Applications to the Trash.
  2. To remove your bottles too, including installed games and their saves, move ~/Library/Application Support/Portnaut to the Trash. In Finder, choose Go › Go to Folder… and paste that path.
  3. If you made save backups, they are in ~/Documents/Portnaut Backups.
